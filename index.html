Whitepapers

At Clarity Medical, innovation is backed by evidence. Explore our collection of peer-reviewed publications, clinical studies, and independent research validating the performance and reliability of our medical technologies.

Source: PGIMER | Springer Nature

PGIMER's laboratory testing published in the Journal of Clinical Monitoring and Computing evaluated pulse oximeters from six leading manufacturers under simulated motion artefacts and low perfusion conditions. Clarity Medical's OxyPlus technology consistently demonstrated high accuracy and reliable performance, maintaining dependable SpO₂ measurements when conventional pulse oximeters may struggle.

Source: BMC Anesthesiology | Springer Nature

Published in BMC Anesthesiology, this first-in-human clinical study validates the safety and oxygen delivery performance of Clarity Medical's indigenously developed TrueOxy+ HFNC system. The study demonstrated predictable oxygenation across multiple FiO₂ levels, excellent participant tolerance and no device-related adverse events.

Source: PGIMER | Sage Journals

This clinical study evaluated the patented CLADS™ automated anaesthesia delivery system using Bispectral Index (BIS) guidance. Compared with manual anaesthesia administration, CLADS™ maintained target anaesthetic depth more consistently, reduced propofol consumption and enabled faster patient recovery, demonstrating the advantages of intelligent closed-loop drug delivery.
